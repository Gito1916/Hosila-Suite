Because Hosila PMS and Hosila IMS keep your property and store records safely on your own Windows computer, keeping regular off-computer backups protects your business against hardware failure or power surges.

## Recommended Backup Routine

- **Automatic Daily Backups:** Hosila PMS saves an automatic property backup during Night Audit, and Hosila IMS saves scheduled store snapshots automatically.
- **External Copy:** Once a day or week, copy your latest backup folder onto a USB flash drive or secondary office computer using the built-in **Settings → Backup & Restore** screen.

## Updating to a Newer Version

Running a newer **Hosila PMS** or **Hosila IMS** installer preserves all existing rooms, guest history, accounts, and store stock automatically. Export a fresh backup before any major update as standard best practice.

## Moving Your License to a Replacement Computer

If you upgrade or replace your main reception or store computer:

1. Install Hosila on the replacement Windows computer and note the new **Machine ID** (`HSLA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) shown on the Activation screen.
2. Restore your latest backup onto the replacement computer so all your records carry over cleanly.
3. Contact Hosila Support via the [Contact Sales & Support form](/contact) (select **Move License to a New Computer**), or message **WhatsApp: 09063814659** / **Hosilateam@gmail.com** with your business name and new Machine ID.
4. Our team will issue your replacement computer's activation key so your remaining license period carries over smoothly.
