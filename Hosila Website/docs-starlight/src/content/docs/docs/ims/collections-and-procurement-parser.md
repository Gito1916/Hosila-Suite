Hosila IMS replaces paper store cards and error-prone spreadsheets with clear two-person accountability for both **outgoing stock** and **incoming market deliveries**.

## Signed Department Stock Issues

When Kitchen, F&amp;B Bar, Housekeeping, or Maintenance requests supplies from Central Stores:

1. Click **Collect Stock** and select the requesting department and staff collector.
2. Add items in any configured unit (`KG`, `Crate`, `Bottle`, `Piece`). Hosila IMS checks shelf availability immediately and prevents issuing more stock than is on hand.
3. Capture both the **Collector** and **Storekeeper** sign-offs.
4. Once posted, stock balances update immediately and department consumption is valued at current average cost.

When unopened drinks or returnable serving trays come back after an event or shift, record a **Stock Return** linked to the original issue so net department usage remains exact.

## Safe Market Excel List Import

Weekly market lists from buyers and suppliers rarely follow one rigid format. Instead of re-typing dozens of lines manually:

1. Open **Procurement → Import Excel List**.
2. Drop any `.xlsx`, `.xls`, or `.csv` market spreadsheet into Hosila IMS.
3. Hosila IMS reads the sheet offline, automatically matches known items, and highlights **sudden price jumps, line total mismatches, unit conversions needed, or new items** behind a mandatory Storekeeper Confirmation screen.
4. Once approved by the storekeeper, Hosila IMS records the received stock and updates average item costs in one clean step.
