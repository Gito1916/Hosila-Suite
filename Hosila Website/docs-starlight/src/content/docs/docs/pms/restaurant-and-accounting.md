Hosila PMS includes an **Integrated Hotel Restaurant & Bar** module built specifically for properties whose dining room, lounge, or room service needs smooth room-bill posting and unified daily close.

> [!NOTE]
> Running an independent restaurant, bar, or retail shop without hotel rooms? See our upcoming standalone **Hosila POS** product instead.

## Menu Items, Kitchen Tickets & Room Posting

- Organize food, drinks, and room-service items by category with clear pricing and optional tax rules.
- Settle orders immediately by **Cash, Card, or Bank Transfer**, or **Post to Room** so the charge appears instantly on the guest&rsquo;s room bill with waiter attribution.
- Print crisp kitchen order dockets and guest receipts directly from the restaurant counter.

## Night Audit & Daily Accounts

At the end of each business day, Reception or the Duty Manager runs **Night Audit** in one guided pass:

1. Flags any unresolved departures, open restaurant tabs, or pending room charges.
2. Posts nightly room rates, taxes, and service charges automatically for all in-house guests.
3. Advances the hotel business date cleanly and updates your daily accounting ledger.

From **Finance & Accounts**, managers and accountants review:

- **Chart of Accounts** across Assets, Liabilities, Revenue, Expenses, and Equity.
- **Daily Journal** entries with permanent reference numbers and staff attribution.
- **Trial Balance**, Profit &amp; Loss summary, Void Audit Report, and one-click spreadsheet exports for external accountants.
