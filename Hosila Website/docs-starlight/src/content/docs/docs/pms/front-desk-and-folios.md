## 1. Fast-Scan Room Board

Every room card shows a clear vertical color bar on the left so receptionists can read the whole hotel in one glance:

- **Mint Green:** Available & Clean — ready for immediate check-in.
- **Charcoal:** Occupied & Clean — active guest stay in good standing.
- **Soft Red:** Dirty, Under Maintenance, or Occupied with an overdue checkout or unpaid balance.

Hosila protects room standards automatically: **checking a guest into a Dirty or Maintenance room is blocked** until Housekeeping or Reception marks it **Clean** inside the side-panel Room Inspector.

## 2. 14/21-Day Booking Chart

Switch to **Reservations** to view two or three weeks of room availability by floor. Click any open date cell to create a reservation pre-filled with that room and arrival date, or click any stay bar to jump straight into the Guest Bill.

## 3. Flexible Guest Billing

- **Extend Stay:** Add extra nights in the same room or move the guest to another clean room, assigning the extra nights to either the **Personal Bill** or **Company Bill**.
- **Move Room:** Relocate an in-house guest mid-stay; the old room automatically switches to **Dirty** for housekeeping, and checkout-morning moves never add accidental extra night charges.
- **Corporate Split Bills & Mixed Payments:** Click **Auto-Split Corporate** to place room charges on the Company invoice and dining/extras on the Personal bill, and accept Cash + Card + Bank Transfer together in one checkout.
- **Settle Later (City Ledger):** Managers and Accountants can authorize checking out VIP or corporate guests with an unpaid balance and a mandatory approval note; all unpaid balances appear cleanly under **Finance → Unpaid Check-Outs**.
