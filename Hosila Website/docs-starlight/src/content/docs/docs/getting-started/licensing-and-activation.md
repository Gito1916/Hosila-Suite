Hosila verifies your license **directly on your computer**—once activated, you never need an internet connection to keep checking in guests or issuing stock. Every commercial license unlocks **100% of all product features**.

## 1. One License Per Property or Store

Each license activates one main computer using the **Machine ID** (`HSLA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) displayed inside Hosila:

- A **Hosila PMS** license activates your main reception computer and automatically covers every additional desk or tablet connected to it over your hotel Wi-Fi.
- A **Hosila IMS** license activates your store computer for full inventory and purchasing workflows.

## 2. How to Pay & Activate Your License

Getting licensed takes just a few minutes:

1. Download and install **Hosila PMS** or **Hosila IMS** from the [Download Centre](/downloads) and explore everything free for 14 days.
2. Open Hosila on your reception or store computer and copy your **Machine ID** (`HSLA-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) from the Activation screen or **Settings → License & Activation**.
3. Fill out our quick [Contact Sales form](/contact) (or message us directly on **WhatsApp: 09063814659** / email **Hosilateam@gmail.com**) with your business name, product, preferred plan, and Machine ID.
4. Once payment is confirmed, our team sends your official activation key directly to you on WhatsApp and email.
5. Paste the activation key into Hosila on your computer—activation finishes immediately, even offline.
