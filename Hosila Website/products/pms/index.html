How It Works At Your Property

One main computer. Your whole property connected.

Simple to set up in your office with zero monthly cloud lag.

1. Main Reception Computer

Install Hosila PMS once on your main reception Windows computer. Every guest folio, room charge, and accounting record stays safely on your premises.

2. Every Desk Connected on Wi-Fi

Additional front-desk laptops, restaurant/bar tablets, and manager offices open Hosila PMS in their browser over your hotel Wi-Fi—seeing room updates and orders instantly.

3. Runs Offline, Emails When Online

Check-ins, room moves, restaurant dockets, and night audit require no internet. Optional guest confirmation and checkout receipt emails send automatically whenever internet is present.
