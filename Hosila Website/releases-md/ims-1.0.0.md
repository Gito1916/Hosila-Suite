### Added
- **Fast Offline Store Control:** Runs directly on your central store Windows computer so kitchen and bar stock issues never wait on internet connectivity.
- **Smart Crate & Bottle Conversions:** Buy in bulk market units (Crates, Bags, Cartons, Packs, Rolls) and issue in single units (Bottles, Kilograms, Litres, Pieces) while Hosila IMS automatically displays bulk plus loose stock and updates average item cost.
- **Fast Counter Stock Issues & Returnables:** Record department stock collections with both Collector and Storekeeper sign-off, support back-dated entries when needed, prevent issuing more stock than exists on the shelf, and track returnable trays or equipment.
- **Market Excel List Import:** Import weekly buyer spreadsheets and automatically flag sudden price jumps, line total mismatches, and unrecognized items for storekeeper review before updating stock balances.

### Audit & Control
- **Permanent Movement History:** Corrections record clear reversal entries with staff attribution rather than silent edits.
- **Automatic Daily Backups:** Protects your store catalog, stock history, and valuation records with scheduled local snapshots.
