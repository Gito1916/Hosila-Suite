### Added
- **Multi-Desk Hotel Wi-Fi Connection:** Install on your main reception computer and let front desk, bar, restaurant, and accounts staff work together live over your property Wi-Fi—with no internet required for daily operations.
- **Fast-Scan Room Board & Slide-Over Inspector:** Clear color-coded room bars (Clean & Available, Occupied, Dirty, or Maintenance) paired with a side-panel Room Inspector that prevents accidental check-ins into dirty rooms.
- **14/21-Day Reservations Chart:** Click any empty date slot to start a booking or click any stay bar to open the Guest Bill directly.
- **Flexible Guest Billing Controls:** Extend stays in the same room or upgrade to a clean room, move rooms mid-stay without billing mistakes, split Company vs. Personal bills in one click, accept mixed payments (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer), approve complimentary waivers, and track **Settle Later (City Ledger)** corporate balances.
- **Integrated Hotel Restaurant Module *(Optional)*:** Manage dining tables, post room-service orders straight to guest room bills, mark items sold out instantly, and print clean kitchen order dockets.

### Financial & Audit Controls
- **Tamper-Proof Daily Accounts:** Every room charge, payment, refund, waiver, and void records a balanced accounting entry automatically.
- **Void Audit Report:** Zero silent deletions—every voided bill requires a written reason and staff attribution, ready to export for management review.
- **Night Audit & Automatic Backups:** Close the business day in one guided pass with automatic daily property backups.
